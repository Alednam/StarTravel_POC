# Plan of Attack: Rigorous Mathematical Physics Engine & Epistemic Synthesis

## Objective
To evolve the Science Calculator from heuristic keyword matching into a **Rigorous Mathematical Physics & Logic Engine**. 

### Epistemic Baseline & Constraint
* **Theoretical Foundation:** Strictly grounded in foundational pre-1984 physics and mathematical literature (Analytical Mechanics, General Relativity, Gauge Field Theory, Non-commutative Operator Algebras, Von Neumann Measurement, Statistical Mechanics, Information Theory, Group Representation Theory).
* **Empirical Integration:** All post-1984 speculative theoretical narratives (e.g., unfalsifiable string landscape models, arbitrary multiverses) are excluded, but **all verified experimental results and mathematical theorems from 1984 to the present are retained as hard constraints** (e.g., Loophole-free Bell inequality violations, Higgs scalar at $125.09\text{ GeV}$, LIGO gravitational wave strain profiles, neutrino mass splittings, precision $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ parameters, Perelman's Ricci flow proof).

## Phase 1: Granular Mathematical Physics Schema (The Atomic Structure)

*Current State:* Theories are treated as loose markdown text parsed by simple string keywords.  
*Target State:* Theories are decomposed into formal mathematical objects with computable attributes.

### 1.1 Formal Schema Definition
Each theory in the engine must define:
1. **State Space / Manifold ($\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{H}, \Gamma$):**
   * Symplectic phase space $(\Gamma, \omega)$, complex Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, or pseudo-Riemannian spacetime $(\mathcal{M}_4, g_{\mu\nu})$.
2. **Symmetry Group & Lie Algebra:**
   * Global and local gauge symmetries (e.g., $\text{ISO}(3,1)$, $SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1)$).
3. **Invariants & Conserved Currents (Noether's Theorem):**
   * Conserved quantities ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$, Casimir operators $P^2, W^2$).
4. **Governing Field Equations / Dynamics:**
   * Differential equations and variational actions ($S = \int \mathcal{L} d^4x$, Euler-Lagrange, Einstein, Dirac, Schrödinger, Lindblad master equations).
5. **Regime of Validity & Scale Bounds:**
   * Cutoffs in energy ($E \ll \Lambda_{\text{UV}}$), velocity ($v \ll c$), action ($S \gg \hbar$), or decoherence time ($t \gg \tau_{\text{dec}}$).
6. **Empirical Benchmarks:**
   * Verified physical constants and experimental datasets against which predictions are tested.
7. **Identified Gaps & Singularities:**
   * Non-renormalizable terms, metric singularities, horizons, measurement boundary problems.

## Phase 2: The Relationship Engine (Connecting the Formalisms)

*Current State:* Graph edges generated by simple keyword bag-of-words overlap.  
*Target State:* Edges represent exact mathematical reductions, gauge extensions, and empirical validations.

### 2.1 Formal Relationship Taxonomy
- **`LIMITING_CASE` (Asymptotic Reduction):** Theory A is an asymptotic limit of Theory B as a parameter vanishes or diverges (e.g., $\lim_{c \to \infty} \text{Relativity} = \text{Newtonian Mechanics}$; $\lim_{\hbar \to 0} \text{QM} = \text{Classical Mechanics}$).
- **`GAUGE_EXTENSION` (Algebraic Embedding):** Theory B embeds or extends the symmetry group of Theory A (e.g., $U(1) \subset SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$).
- **`SUPPORTS` (Empirical & Deductive Validation):** Theory B derives or experimentally verifies a predicted invariant or axiom of Theory A within $\pm N\sigma$.
- **`FILLS_GAP` (Singularity / Anomaly Resolution):** Theory B provides a finite mechanism resolving a mathematical breakdown or divergence in Theory A (e.g., Planck's quantum resolving the ultraviolet catastrophe; Renormalization eliminating QED loop infinities).
- **`CONFLICTS` (Inconsistency / Falsification):** Theory B violates a fundamental constraint of Theory A (e.g., non-unitarity, superluminal signaling, or contradiction with post-1984 empirical data such as Bell test loophole-free results).

### 2.2 Exact Mathematical Complementarity Algorithm
Instead of heuristic keyword counts, complementarity is scored via:

$$\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{T}_A, \mathcal{T}_B) = \mathcal{S}_{\text{domain}}(\mathcal{D}_A, \mathcal{D}_B) \times \left(1 - \mathcal{J}(\mathcal{M}_A, \mathcal{M}_B)\right) \times \kappa(\mathcal{T}_A, \mathcal{T}_B)$$

* $\mathcal{S}_{\text{domain}}$: Compatibility of state space manifolds and boundary conditions.
* $1 - \mathcal{J}$: Orthogonality of mechanisms (rewarding novel mathematical frameworks over redundant ones).
* $\kappa$: Consistency metric enforcing conservation laws, microcausality, and agreement with post-1984 empirical benchmarks.
