# Non-Relativistic Quantum Mechanics & Operator Algebras

> **Core Premise:** The physical state of a system is represented by a ray in a complex Hilbert space, and physical observables are self-adjoint linear operators whose spectral decompositions determine measurement outcome probabilities.

---

## 1. Mathematical Framework & State Space
* **State Space:** Complex separable Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ with inner product $\langle \psi | \phi \rangle$.
* **Algebra of Observables:** Non-commutative $C^*$-algebra $\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$ of bounded and unbounded self-adjoint operators $\hat{A} = \hat{A}^\dagger$.
* **Canonical Commutation Relations (CCR):**
  $$[\hat{x}_i, \hat{p}_j] = i\hbar \delta_{ij} \hat{I}$$

---

## 2. Core Physical Mechanisms
- **Unitary Time Evolution:** Schrödinger equation generated by the system Hamiltonian $\hat{H}$:
  $$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H} |\psi(t)\rangle \implies |\psi(t)\rangle = \hat{U}(t, t_0) |\psi(t_0)\rangle, \quad \hat{U}^\dagger \hat{U} = \hat{I}$$
- **Born Probability Rule & Spectral Projection:** For an observable $\hat{A} = \sum_n a_n \hat{P}_n$, the probability of obtaining eigenvalue $a_n$ on state $\rho$ is:
  $$P(a_n) = \text{Tr}(\hat{\rho} \hat{P}_n)$$
- **Non-Commutative Uncertainty:** Generalized Robertson-Schrödinger inequality:
  $$\sigma_A^2 \sigma_B^2 \ge \frac{1}{4} \left| \langle [\hat{A}, \hat{B}] \rangle \right|^2$$

---

## 3. Formal Axioms
1. **Axiom of State Representation:** Physical states are represented by density matrices $\hat{\rho}$ on $\mathcal{H}$ satisfying $\hat{\rho} \ge 0$ and $\text{Tr}(\hat{\rho}) = 1$.
2. **Axiom of Unitary Kinematics:** Closed system evolution is an automorphism of the state space preserving Hilbert space inner products (Wigner's theorem).
3. **Axiom of Entanglement Non-Locality:** The state space of a composite system is the tensor product of component spaces $\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$, admitting non-separable states $|\Psi_{AB}\rangle \ne |\psi_A\rangle \otimes |\phi_B\rangle$.

---

## 4. Symmetry Groups & Invariants
- **Kinematic Symmetry Group:** Galilei group in non-relativistic regimes; projective representations $U(g)$ on $\mathcal{H}$.
- **Conserved Quantities:** Expectation values of operators commuting with the Hamiltonian $\frac{d}{dt}\langle \hat{O} \rangle = \frac{i}{\hbar} \langle [\hat{H}, \hat{O}] \rangle = 0$.

---

## 5. Post-1984 Empirical Constraints & Benchmarks
- **Loophole-Free Bell Tests (Hensen et al. 2015, Giustina et al. 2015):** Simultaneous closure of locality and detection loopholes, violating Bell-CHSH inequalities:
  $$S_{\text{CHSH}} = 2.42 \pm 0.04 > 2$$
  falsifying local hidden-variable realism.
- **Quantum Teleportation & Entanglement Swapping (Zeilinger et al. 1997+):** Experimental transfer of quantum states between separated systems via entangled pairs and classical communication.
- **Precision $(g-2)_e$ of Electron (Gabrielse et al. 2008):** QED / QM calculation matching electron anomalous magnetic moment to 12 decimal places ($10^{-12}$).

---

## 6. Identified Gaps & Incompatibilities
- **The Measurement Problem:** Discontinuity between continuous deterministic unitary evolution $\hat{U}(t)$ and discontinuous stochastic state reduction $\hat{\rho} \to \hat{P}_n \hat{\rho} \hat{P}_n$.
- **Spacetime Non-Dynamism:** Hilbert space dynamics presupposes a rigid, background-fixed temporal parameter $t$, conflicting with general relativistic dynamical spacetime curvature.
